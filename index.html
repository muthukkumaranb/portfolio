III.Two live demos from the SIH projects

Both run in your browser on real outputs: the first replays SHIKRA's committed model predictions, the second computes real SHA-256 hashes.

Predict an engine's life

SHIKRA

A held-out engine the model never trained or calibrated on. Drag through its life: the band is the conformal 80% interval, and SHIKRA advises removal once the pessimistic edge (q10) reaches R* = 5 cycles.

Tamper with a forecast log

ShadowCat

A registered model, a forecast it made, and the report that cites it. Each entry stores the SHA-256 hash of the one before. Lower the forecast's probability, as someone hiding a missed alert might, and every later entry stops verifying.
